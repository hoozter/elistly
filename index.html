Data & privacy

What we store

When you use an account, we store your account data (email, authentication) and your app data: categories, entity types, entities, settings, and optionally theme preferences. Data is stored in the infrastructure configured for this app.

Why

To provide the app (inventory, workspaces, sync across devices) and to keep your account secure.
