Legal & policies

What the app handles

Account details include your email and display name. Inventory data includes the categories, types, items, settings, and other content you enter. The browser also keeps an account cache and unsent edits locally to support reloads and reconnecting.

Where your information is stored

Elistly uses Cloudflare to run the service and Neon to manage accounts and store your inventory. Your information is not end-to-end encrypted. This means authorised Elistly administrators and service providers may be able to access it when needed to operate, support, protect, or maintain the service.

When you create an account or sign in, your password is sent to Neon to verify your identity. Your password is not stored with your inventory. Neon stores password credentials as a one-way hash rather than as readable plain text.

Your choices

You can export account data in Profile. The Profile menu provides account deletion. Browser-held cache or queued changes are controlled by the browser and device you use.

Deleting your account removes it from the service. Any information still stored in your browser is managed by you through your browser or device settings.

Using Elistly

Elistly is provided free to use in its current form and is in beta. Features and behaviour may change. Do not use it as the only copy of important information.

Use the service lawfully and do not attempt to interfere with it or access another account. You are responsible for the content you enter and for keeping access to your account and devices secure.
